One optimisation job at a time: every trial, the best settings, how the search converged, how two variables interact, and robustness checks (the held-back test, stability around the best, the deflated Sharpe ratio, walk-forward folds).

**Apply to the strategy** writes the best values into the strategy and opens it on the Build page; the variables and their ranges stay.

Saved backtests are compared on the **Backtest results** page.
