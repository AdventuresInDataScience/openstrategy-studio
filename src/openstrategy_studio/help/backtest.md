**Run backtest** runs the current strategy on the stored data. Every order fills at the next bar's open.

- The three tables are the portfolio, its trades and its returns; switch between all metrics, MT5's report and vectorbtX's extras.
- **View** picks one of the charts, drawn in the chart theme beside it.
- A warning box lists anything vectorbtX could not price, such as a short with no borrow rate.
- **Save run** keeps the results; saved runs are listed on Home and can be reopened here.

Compare with buy and hold draws the market itself, held unlevered from the first bar.
