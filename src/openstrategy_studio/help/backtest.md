**Run backtest** runs the current strategy on the stored data. Every order fills at the next bar's open.

- The chart comes first, opening on the equity curve against buy and hold; **View** picks another, drawn in the chart theme beside it. **Log scale** (on the equity curve, the price chart and the Monte Carlo fan, and on the saved runs compared) draws the values on a log axis, so equal percentage moves are the same height: a long history, or an account that grew a lot, reads better that way.
- Under it, the three tables hold every metric: the portfolio, its trades and its returns.
- A warning box lists anything vectorbtX could not price or had to change, such as a short with no borrow rate, or a trade cut down to what the leverage funds, with what to change in the Studio under each.
- **Save run** keeps the results; saved runs are listed on Home, and **Open a saved run** and **Compare saved runs** reopen them here.

Compare with buy and hold draws the market itself, held unlevered from the first bar.
