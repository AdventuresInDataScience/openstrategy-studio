**Optimisation results** shows one job at a time: every trial, the best settings, how the search converged, how two variables interact, and robustness checks (the held-back test, stability around the best, the deflated Sharpe ratio, walk-forward folds).

**Apply to the strategy** writes the best values into the strategy and opens it on the Build page; the variables and their ranges stay.

**Compare saved runs** draws the equity of several saved backtests together.
