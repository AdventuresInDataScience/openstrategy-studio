All data is bars, from one minute to weekly.

- **Download from Yahoo** for symbols such as `SPY`, `VOD.L` or `EURUSD=X`. Intraday history on Yahoo only goes back a limited window.
- **Import a file** (CSV or Parquet, MT4 and MT5 exports included). Every import is checked, and nothing is stored until you accept the report.
- **Refresh or delete** stored data, and store **universe files** that describe many instruments at once.

A strategy can use data at its own timeframe or any finer one; finer bars are combined up.
